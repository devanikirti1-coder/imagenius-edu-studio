02 About the Studio

Sixteen years
of teaching the line.

Imagenius Edu. Studio was founded by Deep Rajpara as a place where interior design is taught the way architects learn to think — slowly, by hand, by drawing, before any software is ever opened.

Sixteen years on, more than twelve hundred students have come through the studio. Some now lead practices of their own; others sit beside the founder, still drawing.
