04 Software — the tools of the trade

Taught the way working studios use them.

i AutoCAD 2D drafting

The industry standard for precise plans, sections, and elevations — the drawing language every brief begins in.

Foundation · 80 hrs
ii SketchUp Quick 3D massing

The fastest way to think in three dimensions. Massing studies, volume, scale — and the early model every project lives in.

Foundation · 60 hrs
iii 3ds Max Detailed modelling

Where geometry meets cinematic detail. Hard-surface modelling, materials, and the foundation for V-Ray's photoreal output.

Advanced · 100 hrs
iv Lumion Real-time render

Walkthroughs, weather, and immersive client presentations — built for fast, expressive scenes.

Practice · 40 hrs
v V-Ray Photoreal render

Light, glass, marble, fabric — rendered the way the human eye expects them. The portfolio renderer of choice.

Advanced · 80 hrs
vi Photoshop Mood & postwork

Concept boards, atmosphere, and the final composition of every render. The quiet finish behind every great image.

Foundation · 50 hrs
vii Revit BIM workflow

For coordinating real construction — schedules, sections, and the parametric way modern studios document a project.

Advanced · 70 hrs
viii Enscape Live visualisation

In-model real-time rendering — iterate at the speed of thought, while the client is still in the room.

Practice · 30 hrs
